5-Field Agent Role Specifications (U4-1)

Strict multi-agent role separation guaranteeing modularity and bounded responsibilities.

1. TriageSupervisor Coordinator

Goal: Classify complaints using 3x self-consistency voting and enforce MAX_HOPS=3.

Tools: ChatOllama (temp=0.7), SQLite history loader, Chroma retriever.

I/O: In: Complaint, turn. Out: Category, votes, reasoning.

Limits: Never generates customer replies; bounded by hop count.

2. BillingAgent Specialist

Goal: Resolve duplicate charges, refunds, and invoices using corporate policy SLA.

Tools: ChatOllama (temp=0), Chroma refund policy FAQ, SQLite history.

I/O: In: Complaint, RAG policy, thread. Out: Refund timeline reply.

Limits: Cannot advise on technical faults or issue credits directly.

3. TechnicalAgent Specialist

Goal: Diagnose app crashes, bugs, and login errors; collect diagnostic telemetry.

Tools: ChatOllama (temp=0), Chroma troubleshooting FAQ.

I/O: In: Complaint, FAQ steps. Out: Diagnostic advice & info request.

Limits: Cannot access billing records or alter payment states.

4. GeneralAgent Specialist

Goal: Acknowledge feedback, praise, and out-of-scope inquiries.

Tools: ChatOllama (temp=0), Chroma contact directory.

I/O: In: Complaint, contact FAQ. Out: Polite response & SLA note.

Limits: Grounded: answers "I don't know" to unanswerable queries.

5. HumanReviewer HITL Oversight

Goal: Audit angry complaints, legal threats, and ambiguous classifications.

Tools: LangGraph interrupt() review interface.

I/O: In: Draft response, reason. Out: APPROVE or REJECT.

Limits: Human gatekeeper requiring explanatory rejection note.
